Every genus in Open Paleo now has an infobox you can add to your own website, blog or class page.
It shows the genus's name and who named it, its age, a short description and a few facts such as
its diet and size, with an outline where we have one.

Here's the one for <i class="taxon">Tyrannosaurus</i>:

## How to add one

Open the genus's page and scroll to "Embed this genus". Copy the code there and paste it into your
page's HTML. The infobox links back to the genus's page on Open Paleo and carries its own credit:
the data's license and the outline's credit line.

The frame is 400 × 570 pixels, so a longer infobox scrolls inside it. If you'd like the frame to
grow to fit, the [developer documentation](/developers/#fit-the-frame) has a short script for that.

## What we collect

Each time someone views an infobox on your site, we count it as a visit, as we do for pages on
Open Paleo. We don't set cookies, and we don't keep anything that identifies your readers. For the
details, see our [privacy policy](/privacy/).
